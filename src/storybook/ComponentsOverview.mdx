# Components

Every WMDS export, grouped by what it is for. The sidebar lists the same components A–Z, and related exports share a family folder: **Button** (Button, FloatingActionButton, IconButton), **Card** (Card, SelectableCard), **Checkbox** (Checkbox, CheckboxGroup), and **Radio** (Radio, RadioGroup).

Each component page follows **Usage → Anatomy → Best practices → Examples**. When a **Pattern — …** story matches your task, copy its **Show code**.
